    Model assumptions and limits
    • The room is well mixed. One concentration represents the whole space; real rooms form gradients near people, windows and filters.
    • CO2 and quanta exchange counts natural ventilation, mechanical outdoor air and infiltration (infiltration ACH times volume). PM2.5 uses the same exchange with a 70% particle penetration factor through cracks.
    • Natural ventilation is a teaching estimate. Wind uses discharge coefficients 0.25 single-sided and 0.62 cross-flow with two equal openings; stack uses a fixed neutral height; the two combine by root-sum-square. Real openings, screens and gusts differ.
    • Occupancy follows a fixed five-period schedule and emission rates are constant per person: 18 L/h of CO2 equivalent per student and a set quanta rate per infectious person.
    • Wells-Riley risk scales linearly from one integrated unit-source concentration, so masks, infectious count and quanta rate multiply the dose exactly. It is a relative risk teaching tool, not an infection forecast.
    • MERV-13 is modeled as 55% particle removal on the incoming mechanical outdoor air stream plus a 4 ACH equivalent recirculation credit and a 40 W fan-energy penalty; it is passive and adds no noise. The intake filter cuts incoming PM2.5 only; it adds no infection-control clean air on top of the outdoor exchange already counted. UVGI adds a 3 ACH equivalent removal. Fan boxes and HEPA units add fixed clean-air delivery rates.
    • Noise is the logarithmic sum of independent fan sources (52 dBA per fan box, 49 dBA per HEPA unit). Costs are rough single-number USD estimates for comparison only.
    • Annualized retrofit costs in the comparator are disclosed planning estimates: capital amortized over device life, filter or lamp consumables each year, and fan electricity at $0.16/kWh over 1,350 occupied hours (7.5 h x 180 school days). They are for comparison, not quotes.
    • The engineering brief signature is a SHA-256 integrity digest computed in the page. It proves a file was not edited after export; it is tamper evidence, not a cryptographic signature by an author.
    • All scenarios are synthetic reference models. Nothing here is a measurement, a pilot, or a claim about a real building.
